Privacy policy

ShortForge, the publishing tool of Under the Trend. Effective 23 November 2026.

Who this covers

ShortForge is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It has no other users and no sign-up. It does not collect data about viewers or members of the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, given through Google's and Meta's own sign-in screens, ShortForge accesses:

  • YouTube upload: to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel.
  • YouTube read-only: the owner's channel id and name, to confirm the upload goes to the right channel, and the status of videos it uploaded.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only: views, watch time and retention of the owner's own videos.
  • Instagram: the owner's account id and username, permission to publish Reels to that account, and basic figures for the posts it published.

It does not read email, contacts, comments from other people, or any other account's data.

How it is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and are never published or shared. Analytics figures are stored in a local database on that computer and used only to choose and improve future videos.

Sharing

ShortForge does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Data is sent only to the platform it came from (for example, a video and its title are sent to YouTube to be published).

Google API Services

ShortForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ShortForge uses YouTube API Services; by using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account permissions (YouTube) and in Instagram's settings under Apps and websites (Instagram). The owner can delete the stored tokens and analytics from the local computer at any time. To ask about data, write to the contact below.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
