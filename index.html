ShortForge

The publishing tool behind the Under the Trend channel.

What it is

ShortForge is a private tool run by one person, the owner of Under the Trend. It makes short, fact-checked explainer videos (30 to 60 seconds, vertical) about science and everyday concepts. It then uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up. It connects only to accounts that the owner controls.

What it does with Google and YouTube

  • Uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel, with title, description and sources.
  • Reads the owner's channel details to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.
  • Reads view and retention figures for the owner's own videos, to decide which topics work.

Every video is marked as AI-generated (altered or synthetic content) on YouTube and Instagram, and carries an on-screen AI label.
